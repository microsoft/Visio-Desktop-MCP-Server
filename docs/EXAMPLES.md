# Example prompts

Use the Visio tools in your connected AI client. Give file paths and page names
when working with existing drawings.

## Create

> Open Visio and create an org chart with a CEO, Engineering and Sales managers,
> and two reports under each manager. Save the drawing and tell me its path.

## Understand

> Open `C:\Diagrams\approval.vsdx`. Summarize the steps and decision branches on
> the Approval page. Point out anything you cannot determine from the drawing.

## Make a focused edit

> In `C:\Diagrams\approval.vsdx`, rename "Manager review" to "Finance review" on
> the Approval page. Keep all other content and layout unchanged. Save a copy as
> `C:\Diagrams\approval-updated.vsdx`.

## Check and export

> Check the Approval page for overlapping shapes and unconnected connectors.
> Explain the findings without changing the drawing, then export that page to
> `C:\Diagrams\approval.png`.
